## Definitions

- **"WordCore"** means the software published by the WordCore project: the `wordcore` program, the core, the official addons and themes, and the tools in this repository.
- **"WordCore Interfaces"** means the documented ways addons and themes work with WordCore: the `wc` API, hooks, routes, shortcodes, templates and theme regions, the admin UI kit, and the manifest and repository index formats.
- **"Independent Addon or Theme"** means an addon or theme that:
  1. is a separate package, installed into WordCore rather than built into it, and
  2. works with WordCore only through the WordCore Interfaces, and
  3. does not contain WordCore's own code, apart from code examples copied from WordCore's documentation.

You may distribute an Independent Addon or Theme together with WordCore, for example in a site package or a hosting image. WordCore stays under GPLv3 and the addon or theme stays under its own licence.

## What this does not change

- WordCore itself, and any modified version of it, stays under GPLv3. This exception does not let you distribute WordCore or changes to WordCore under other terms.
- An addon or theme that includes or modifies WordCore's own code is not an Independent Addon or Theme, and GPLv3 applies to it as normal.
- Third-party software included in WordCore (for example Lua, SQLite, zlib, libssh2, mbedTLS, libwebp) stays under its own licence.
